# Business Problem and Justification

Manual library processes can cause delays in book discovery, checkout/return, overdue tracking, inventory accuracy, and report preparation. A centralized platform can provide searchable catalog records, member management, circulation, fine calculations, notifications, and reporting.

## Intended outcomes
- Faster book searches and checkout processing.
- More consistent inventory and borrowing records.
- Easier overdue tracking and reporting.

The assignment provides example targets (search under 5 seconds, checkout processing improved by 80%, overdue returns reduced by 40%, record accuracy 99.9%). These are targets to validate, not achieved results.
