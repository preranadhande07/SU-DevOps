# Design Thinking Report

## Empathize
Study students, faculty, librarians, and administrators as they search, borrow, return, track fines, and prepare reports.

## Define
Library members experience delays locating and borrowing resources due to manual tracking; staff lack centralized real-time visibility into inventory and circulation.

## Ideate
Consider catalog search, email/SMS reminders, QR membership cards, mobile self-checkout, inventory dashboards, and automatic fine calculation. Prioritize ideas by value and feasibility.

## Prototype
Create low-fidelity wireframes for Login, Catalog Search, Librarian Checkout, and Admin Analytics.

## Test
Ask students and library staff to try the prototype, observe task completion and confusion, record feedback, and revise the design.
