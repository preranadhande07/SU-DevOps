# Stakeholders and User Personas

| Group | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Business: college management, library admin, students, faculty | Define goals and library policies; validate usability |
| Engineering: developers, UX designers, QA | Design, implement, and test the product |
| Operations: DevOps, database admins, security | Provision infrastructure, protect data, automate delivery, monitor service |

## Personas
- **Aarav (Student):** wants fast book searches and clear due dates; struggles with queues and unexpected fines.
- **Mrs. Sunita (Librarian):** wants fast checkout and accurate inventory; struggles with manual entries and stock discrepancies.
- **Dr. Verma (Administrator):** wants usage analytics and reports; struggles with delayed, manual reporting.
