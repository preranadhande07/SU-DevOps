# Library Domain Analysis

## Core operations
1. Book acquisition and cataloging (ISBN, authors, categories, shelf location, copy count).
2. Membership management and borrowing limits.
3. Search, issue, renew, return, and due-date tracking.
4. Overdue fines and receipt/payment handling if in scope.
5. Usage, overdue, loss, and replenishment reporting.

## Rules to confirm with stakeholders
- Borrowing limits by role; loan duration and renewal rules.
- Fine rate and maximum fine.
- Lost/damaged book process.
- Whether reservations and payments are in the MVP.
- Privacy and record-retention requirements.
